# v0.4.0 Experimental BSC Testnet Checkpoint

## Status

**BSC TESTNET ONLY — CHECKPOINT, NOT FINAL CERTIFICATION**

This checkpoint records the currently demonstrated integrated capital and
settlement cycle. It is not Mainnet ready, production ready, a final audit, or
final economic certification. Migration testing, deeper economic-cycle tests,
additional settlement tests, and final reconciliation remain pending.

## Demonstrated integration

- `10,000 TestUSDT` investment capital accepted.
- `2 TestUSDT` protocol revenue recorded.
- `10,000 AVS` minted.
- `5%` of capital allocated to Marketplace liquidity.
- `95%` of capital forwarded as productive Trading capital.
- One `+100 TestUSDT` net realized mock trading settlement recorded from
  `110 TestUSDT` gross PnL and `10 TestUSDT` fees.
- `90 TestUSDT` allocated to holder NAV.
- `10 TestUSDT` allocated to the buyback reserve.
- Replay protection demonstrated for settlement ID, execution hash, and
  sequence.
- No physical TestUSDT movement occurred during the accounting settlement.
